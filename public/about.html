About DiveSea

DiveSea is a demo NFT marketplace built as a study project. It shows how a modern marketplace works: you can browse artworks, bid in auctions, buy NFTs instantly and keep them in your personal collection.

What you can do

  • Create an account and get 10 ETH of virtual balance
  • Place bids on auction items
  • Buy NFTs instantly and see them in your profile
  • Change your nickname and profile photo

Technology

The front end is written in HTML5, CSS (Flexbox, Grid, variables, media queries) and vanilla JavaScript. The back end is Node.js with Express and an SQLite database.
