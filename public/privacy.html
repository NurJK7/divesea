Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

When you sign up, DiveSea stores your username, nickname, an optional profile photo and a salted hash of your password. We never store your password in plain text.

How we use it

Your data is used only to run your account: to sign you in, show your profile, keep your virtual balance and record your bids and purchases.

Cookies

We use one session cookie to keep you logged in. It is removed when you log out.

Your choices

You can change your nickname and photo at any time on the profile page. To delete your account, contact us through the Contact page.

DiveSea is a demo marketplace: balances are virtual and no real money or cryptocurrency is used.
